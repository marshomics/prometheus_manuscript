## Verified correct, no change

- geNomad output layout `{outdir}/{stem}_summary/{stem}_virus_summary.tsv` / `_virus.fna` with stem = staged
  `{genome}` (compressed inputs drop one more suffix): the rule's declared outputs match.
- geNomad never reports a contig both as a whole virus and as a provirus host (`flag_sequences` keeps the two
  sets disjoint); several proviruses per contig are allowed. `seq_name` = first header token, while
  `_virus.fna` keeps the full header: `rloop_predict.py` and CheckV (`header.split()[0]`) both key on the first
  token, so the three per-genome tables join exactly (asserted in `aggregate_viral.py`).
- QmRLFS two-decimal rounding never changes a REZ (L <= 2000) or RIZ decision for integer G counts (exhaustive
  check of all (g, L)); the rounding guard is kept as insurance.
- phyloglm accepts non-ultrametric ML trees (it extends tips to the mean depth), needs branch lengths and named
  rows; summary columns and Wald p conditional on alpha as used. BH `p_adj` recomputed independently in the
  test.
- The R-loop association is two-sided on purpose: Prometheus could be retained under R-loop-forming phage
  pressure (positive) or purge R-loop-forming proviruses (negative); both are documented in the script header.
- Real archaeal virus inputs behave sensibly: SSV1 (an integrating archaeal virus, 40 % GC) has no RLFS; the
  GC-rich haloarchaeal SH1 has 25 calls in 13 loci, which motivates the GC/length-adjusted sensitivity estimate
  reported for `frac_rloop_proviruses`.

## Not verifiable here / limitations (see the leaf result `unresolved`)

- geNomad and CheckV were not executed (not installed; databases are multi-GB downloads). Their interfaces were
  verified from the pinned sources and exercised through stubs that write the verified formats and reproduce the
  verified failure modes (crash, missing DB, CheckV exit-0-without-output).
- The reference QmRLFS-finder could not be re-run on the final hand-built constructs (`rloop_synthetic.fna`; the
  run was blocked by the sandbox safety check); those constructs are asserted against hand-derived answers,
  and reference equivalence rests on the real genomes plus the first synthetic set (83 calls).
- Design 6.2 (within-methanogen niche model with methanogenesis subtype) is not implemented; a
  methanogen-adjusted partial effect is reported as a sensitivity column for every niche term.
